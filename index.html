Plan

    Write it however you like. 12x10s is 12 sets of 10 seconds. Add rest with a slash or words: 8x20s/10s, 10 rounds of 45 seconds work 15 seconds rest. Chain parts with commas or "then": 5m warmup, 6x1:30/30s, 3m cooldown. Program a routine as rounds-work-rest groups split by slashes: 6-10-8/4-10-8/2-10-8 is 6 rounds of 10s work and 8s rest, then 4 rounds, then 2. Add a fourth number for a longer rest after a group: 6-10-8-60/4-10-8. Repeat a group with parentheses: 3x(8x20s/10s, 1m rest). Name moves: plank 1m, rest 15s, side plank 30s. Shortcuts: tabata, emom 10. Tap Save to keep a routine on this phone. Once a workout starts, the screen switches to a large clock; tap it to pause, and press Reset to get back to editing. Tap any interval in the plan to jump to it.
